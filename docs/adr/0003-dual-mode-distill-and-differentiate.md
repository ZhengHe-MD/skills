# Dual-mode scope: distill prior art, differentiate on the gaps

**Status: superseded by [0006](./0006-split-too-ai-and-translate.md)** — kept for the prior-art research it recorded; the dual-mode design itself was reconsidered.

The skill covers two situations in one unified, mode-detecting flow: translating source-language text into Chinese, and polishing already-Chinese text (hand-written or agent-generated) that reads as AI 味儿. This was a real choice, not the default: mature prior art already exists (`MrGeDiao/shuorenhua`, 1.2k★, actively maintained) that covers polish-only well, and adopting it outright was on the table. We chose to distill its converged rules (banned phrases, style techniques) rather than re-derive them, but also to differentiate rather than adopt wholesale, because shuorenhua explicitly excludes translation-from-source from its scope, and nothing found in the space addresses cross-agent capability gaps — both of which are the user's actual daily pain point. Pure adoption was also rejected because shuorenhua's shape (scene detection, tiered severity, mandatory two-pass reread, protected-spans rules) is a system, not a Pocock-style concise skill.
