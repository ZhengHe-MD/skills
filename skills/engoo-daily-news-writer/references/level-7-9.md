# Levels 7-9 — Advanced to proficient

**Article:** ~310 words at Level 7, ~345 at Level 8, ~350 at Level 9.

- **Level 7** — journalistic and professional: complex sentences with multiple clauses, sophisticated transitions, passive voice and conditionals, and data the student can point at (percentages, dates, quotes). Finance, technology, international relations.
- **Level 8** — the same register at higher density: advanced business and political terminology, abstract concepts, serious news.
- **Level 9** — academic and formal throughout: sophisticated sentence variety, formal register, politics and economics and other complex subjects.

**Vocabulary:** business and technical terminology with nuanced shades of meaning at Levels 7-8; specialized terminology and abstract nouns at Level 9 — *insurrection*, *martial law*, *proclamation*.

**Sequence:** Vocabulary → Article → Discussion → Further Discussion.

These levels run no Questions section — the lesson goes straight from the article to discussion. Leave `{{QUESTIONS_SECTION}}`, `{{FILL_IN_BLANKS_SECTION}}`, and `{{WOULD_YOU_RATHER_SECTION}}` empty.
