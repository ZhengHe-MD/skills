# Levels 5-6 — Intermediate

**Article:** ~250 words at Level 5, ~300 words at Level 6. Informative but accessible, with no subheadings — one coherent narrative. Level 5 introduces compound sentences and mixes short with medium ones, over varied tenses, some passive voice, and basic transitions. Level 6 adds statistics, historical background, and nuanced explanation, carried by longer transitions and more varied sentence lengths.

**Vocabulary:** common academic words, simple idioms, and everyday phrasal verbs at Level 5; broader academic vocabulary, more idioms and phrasal verbs, and topic-specific terms at Level 6.

**Sequence:** Vocabulary → Article → Questions → Discussion → Further Discussion.

## `{{QUESTIONS_SECTION}}`

Three questions at either level, testing recall of specific facts from the article. The format differs.

Level 5 — multiple choice, two options each:

```html
<section id="questions">
    <h2>Questions</h2>
    <ol class="questions-list">
        <li>How many seats does the cinema have? A. 28 B. 48</li>
        <li>Where is the new cinema located? A. In the city center B. At the airport</li>
        <li>When did it open? A. Last month B. Last week</li>
    </ol>
</section>
```

Level 6 — open-ended short answer:

```html
<section id="questions">
    <h2>Questions</h2>
    <ol class="questions-list">
        <li>What percentage of kindergarten children in Japan had cavities in the latest government survey?</li>
        <li>How many times per day do Japanese children brush their teeth on average?</li>
        <li>What program has helped improve dental health in schools?</li>
    </ol>
</section>
```
