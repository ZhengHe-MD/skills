# Highlights

Retain exactly **one continuous practice clip per requested routine**, in source order, plus suitable opening and ending shots. A clip may contain several repetitions or rallies. Treat side variations as part of one routine unless the user asks to represent them separately.

## Compare candidates

When an earlier training record exists, use its routine map and retained intervals to find candidates. Check their source-time mapping before reuse. Otherwise shortlist from the whole-source review. Avoid selecting the first acceptable occurrence by default.

For each routine, compare the plausible strongest bouts across its occurrence in the session. Inspect finalists densely enough to follow consecutive movement patterns and outcomes; use playback or closer frames wherever still images leave uncertainty. Compare:

- Sustained rhythm and recognizable, completed drill patterns.
- Fewer aborted attempts, interruptions, and visible errors.
- Clear focus, unobstructed view, and the player's complete movement area in frame.
- A coherent beginning and ending suitable for one continuous cut.

Choose the strongest visible example overall. Length alone does not establish quality; a shorter clean bout can be better than a longer interrupted one. Keep its repetitions and ordinary recovery intact instead of stitching isolated successes together. Make editorial selections without inventing technical scores or unverified successful-shot counts.

Record the selected candidate and a brief comparison reason for each routine. If there is only one usable bout, inspect and use it. If none is usable, identify the missing routine explicitly rather than substituting a different drill or manufacturing a clip.

## Coverage check

Match the final selection to the requested routine checklist: one practice interval per routine, no duplicate routine selected merely to show another side, and all selections in source order. Opening and ending are separate bookends, not extra routines. Preserve warm-up subjects when the user requests the full session structure.

Use the original source for newly trimmed selections where available. Exact intervals already encoded for the training record can be reused under the compatibility checks in the media reference reached from the main workflow.
